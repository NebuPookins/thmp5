## How to Contribute

By submitting a pull request, you agree to license your contribution
under the MIT license, and you agree that your contribution may be
relicensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (or any
later version) for inclusion in this project.

### Why this arrangement?

This is primarily a personal project and I don't expect many (or any)
contributions. For now, I want to retain maximum flexibility — the
project as a whole is AGPL (so others can only use it under strong
copyleft terms), but contributions come in under MIT (so they can be
relicensed later if ever needed). If this arrangement deters you from
contributing, reach out — I'm happy to talk through the tradeoffs and
could be convinced to relicense.
